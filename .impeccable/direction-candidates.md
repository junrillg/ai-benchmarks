# Direction derivation

The mechanism is a source-backed comparison of AI capability and measured task cost, with one readable graph per benchmark. This is an Operate surface: the user inspects a benchmark, selects models, and follows evidence. The supplied charts pin the graph format and animation, not the rest of the brand.

The audience scene is a reader comparing models in a browser on a laptop. Daylight-friendly chart surfaces with readable axis labels serve this scene. No audience-specific claims are inferred.

Category rut: a generic dark dashboard of KPI cards. Predictable opposite: a cream editorial landing page whose charts are below a marketing hero.

Seven complete directions, ordered by resonance:

1. Scientific figure plates: a cool white research canvas, blue typographic hierarchy, plotted results occupying the first viewport, source notes in a precise right column. Repeated figure labels carry the workflow across all benchmarks.
2. Observatory atlas: fog blue sectional navigation, cobalt plots, navy labels and a wide single graph with model catalog beneath. A small overview map locates the current capability domain.
3. Transit route diagrams: crisp white, indigo wayfinding and provider-color route lines; effort levels become readable stops on the cost axis, with model controls in a narrow rail. The graph itself is the navigation landmark.
4. Precision calibration studio: pale sage controls, dark green typography, brass selection accents, graph as a measured instrument and an explicit calibration/source strip below. Numeric controls remain familiar.
5. Aviation comparison worksheet: silver gray report ground, navy text and amber active controls, a broad chart beside a concise readings table. Evidence and conditions resemble an operations checklist without simulated gauges.
6. Race telemetry desk: charcoal page, white plot field, tightly arranged data labels, cobalt and vermilion series. The model selector and graph share one instrument frame; task cost remains a real axis rather than a made-up speed metric.
7. Product teardown index: pale silver page, graphite text and emerald focus, vertical benchmark index beside a chart and expanding result ledger. The first viewport shows the comparison mechanism with minimal introduction.

These span scientific print graphics, cartographic navigation, physical instrumentation and operations screen traditions. All preserve real SVG chart semantics and source truth.
